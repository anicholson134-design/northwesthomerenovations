About us

Specialists in transforming your home

North West Home Renovations is a family-run renovation company based in Wigan, working across Lancashire and the North West since 1995. We take tired, awkward or simply dated homes and turn them into spaces that work for the people living in them.

Every trade you need is in-house, from the builders who do the structural work to the plumbers, electricians, plasterers, tilers and decorators who finish it. You deal with one person, you get one written quote with no hidden costs, and you know exactly who is responsible for the result.
